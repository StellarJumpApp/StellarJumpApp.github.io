Stellar Jump – Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026

Stellar Jump is a single-player arcade game for iPhone made by Alim Khakimov ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: the game has no accounts, asks for nothing personal, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information the app stores on your device

Your best score, orb balance, unlocked characters and visual styles, and your settings (sound, effects, visual pack) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and shares no payment details with us. To deliver what you bought, the app receives a signed purchase record from Apple that contains a transaction identifier and the product purchased. The app keeps a short list of recent transaction identifiers on your device so a purchase is never credited twice. Purchases can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Information collected by Unity

Stellar Jump is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's In-App Purchasing and Unity Services packages. When the app runs, these packages may send Unity the following technical data: a device identifier generated by the app, device model and operating system version, app version, country or region, and purchase events (product, price, currency and whether the purchase succeeded). Unity uses this to operate the purchasing service and to provide aggregated analytics such as install and purchase counts. This data is not linked to your name or contact details and is not used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Unity's own policy is at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or name is requested.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no advertising identifier.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No sale of personal information.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases on a child's device go through Apple's Ask to Buy when it is enabled by the family organizer.

Your choices

You can delete all locally stored data by deleting the app. Purchase history is held by Apple and can be viewed in your Apple Account settings.
