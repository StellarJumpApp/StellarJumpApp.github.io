STELLAR JUMP

Calm one-tap neon arcade

Tap left. Tap right. Climb. Launch through the gaps between glowing bars, keep your FLOW going and see how high you can get. No timer, no difficulty spikes, no ads, and it plays anywhere, even offline.

What it is

Flow, not stress

Chain clean gates for a FLOW multiplier, skim bars for near-miss bonuses, bounce off the walls. The difficulty never ramps up.

28 visual styles

Neon, watercolor, comic book, cross-stitch, stained glass, a handheld LCD, gold leaf, a living microscope slide and more.

11 heroes

From one-hit Spark to Phoenix, who rises again, and Tadpole, whose tail traces every jump. Each plays differently.

5 power-ups

Wide Open, Slow Time, Magnet, Double Up and Phase. They stack, so two at once combine.

21 sectors

Collect a gem and the world shifts into a new sector with its own colours and its own calm music.

Made of code

Every bar, hero, sound and song is generated by code. Up to 120 fps on ProMotion iPhones and iPads.

Questions

Does it have ads?

No ads of any kind. Everything unlocks by playing; optional orb packs and a one-time Unlock Everything purchase are there if you want to go faster.

Does it work offline, without Wi-Fi?

Yes. The whole game plays offline, including in airplane mode. A connection is only needed to buy something.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 15.0 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon through the App Store. Game controllers work too.

Is it on Android?

Not yet.
